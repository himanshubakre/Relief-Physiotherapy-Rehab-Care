About the clinic

Recovery, planned around your everyday movement

Relief Physiotherapy Rehab Care is a neighbourhood physiotherapy clinic in Dahisar West, working with patients on pain relief, injury recovery, and regaining strength and mobility after surgery or illness. Care is led by Dr. Shubham Mishra (PT), with a focus on orthopedic, neurological and sports rehabilitation.

What we treat

Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Services

Orthopedic Rehabilitation

Support for joint, bone and muscle conditions, including back, neck and knee pain.

Neurological Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy support for neurological conditions, including recovery after stroke.

Sports Injury Recovery

Rehabilitation for sports-related injuries, aimed at a safe return to activity.

Post-Operative Recovery

Guided rehabilitation to help restore movement and strength after surgery.

Pain Management

Assessment-led care for chronic and acute pain, with a plan tailored to your concern.

Exercise-Based Rehabilitation

Structured, guided exercise programmes to rebuild strength, flexibility and balance.
